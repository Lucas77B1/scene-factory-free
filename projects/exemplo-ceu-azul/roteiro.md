# exemplo-ceu-azul

- **Style:** caderno
- **Format:** vertical

## Idea

Why the sky is blue during the day and orange at sunset: it's the same Sun, and the air scatters the blue.

## Script

Look up on a sunny day: why is the sky blue, and not white?
Sunlight looks white, but it carries all the colors together.
When it enters the air, it hits molecules too small to see.
And blue is the color that scatters the most, in every direction.
That's why, wherever you look, blue reaches your eyes.
At sunset, the light crosses so much more air to reach you.
The blue gets lost on the way, and orange and red are left.
The same Sun, painting the sky in two different colors.
